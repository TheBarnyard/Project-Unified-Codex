Falling Pendulum Solver

One arm on a fixed pivot, let go at rest from θ₀. θ is measured from straight up, clockwise positive. Every number below is solved live with 4th-order Runge–Kutta (0.2 ms step), so viscous and Coulomb friction are exact, not approximated.
